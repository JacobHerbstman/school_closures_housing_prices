\section*{September 22, 2026: Flagging quick resales}
\textit{Drafted by Claude; Jacob decided to flag these sales for robustness rather than drop them.}

About one clean sale in eight comes within a year of the same parcel's
previous recorded sale: 14,831 of 124,396 in 2008--2018. The median resale price is 2.4 times
the earlier price, and the median gap is 213 days. Many of these are flips.
The assessor's characteristics do not record renovation between the sales, so
property controls cannot absorb it. The county's repeat flag removes only
resales at the same price. For 59\% of flagged sales, the previous sale is not
in the price sample, mostly because it was foreclosure-related. These resales
could also reflect closure-driven neighborhood change, so they remain in the
main sample. \texttt{resale\_within\_365} marks them, and the gap columns allow
other windows, since published studies use windows from 3 days to a year.

The flagged share peaks at 16.5\% in 2010 and falls to about 10\% after 2014.
In the half-mile single-family packet, 14.9\% of treated and 18.9\% of control
sales are flagged in 2008--2012. The shares are 10.7\% and 14.2\% in
2014--2018. The flagged share falls by similar amounts in both groups.
Most pre-2013 dates are known only to the month, so those gaps are approximate. No
regression has been rerun.
