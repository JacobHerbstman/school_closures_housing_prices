\section*{September 8, 2026: Extending the overview through 2023}
\textit{Drafted by Codex.}

The housing inputs support extending the descriptive quarter-mile packet
through 2023. The 2018 endpoint was an analysis window, not the end of available
sales. The extended packet contains 18,145 transactions: 5,592 near treated
schools and 12,553 near controls. It adds 6,545 sales in 2019--2023 and retains
all 11,600 earlier transactions. The 2008--2018 annual price summaries match
the earlier packet. Each added year contains sales in all twelve months.

School definitions, school locations, distance exclusions, eligible property
types, and county sale-quality rules remain fixed. Missing characteristics
are still allowed in descriptive trends. For each added year, the same annual
99.9th-percentile price-per-square-foot cutoff is calculated from citywide
sales with complete characteristics and then applied where floor area is
observed. Existing earlier-year cutoffs do not change. Dollar prices remain
in 2022 purchasing power. Recorded characteristics are available throughout,
although the legacy home-improvement correction applies only before 2021.
Later reuse of school buildings does not change treatment assignment.

The longer series shows that the treated mean's 2016--2018 decline is followed
by a rebound: from \$293,567 in 2018 to \$393,817 in 2021, then \$324,669 in
2023. The control mean is \$229,863, \$299,990, and \$273,899 in those years.
These transaction means combine price movements with changes in properties
and neighborhoods supplying sales. The previous composition findings remain
specific to 2016--2018 and have not been extended to explain the later changes.
