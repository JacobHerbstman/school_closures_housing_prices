\section*{September 22, 2026: Buildings vacated by relocating welcoming schools}
\textit{Drafted by Claude; found in the September 22 code review and confirmed from the school records.}

Fourteen welcoming schools moved into a closed school's building for 2013--14.
The design already treats this correctly for the closed school: its building
stayed in use, so it is not one of the 30 treated programs. But each move
vacated the welcoming school's own 2012--13 building. For nearby homes, that
building emptied just as a treated building did. The distance task placed
welcoming schools only at their 2013--14 locations, so the old buildings were
missing. Nine of the old buildings are candidate sites, because those
welcoming schools were themselves on the February list, and nearby sales were
already excluded. The other five were invisible: Wentworth, Leland, Burnham,
Pershing East and Courtenay.

These buildings are now a separate location role, and the exposure file
records the nearest one, its distance, and the number within a quarter mile.
At a quarter mile, 82 treated sales near Armstrong are also near Leland's old
building, and 9 control sales near Hinton are near Wentworth's. At a half
mile, 380 control sales and 8 treated sales are near a vacated building. For
the half-mile comparison this matters most, because those control homes
experienced the event that defines treatment. Excluding them, like sales near
welcoming schools and other candidates, is a regression-stage choice not yet
made. The school cleaner now also fails if an assigned welcoming school lacks
either location; Montefiore's 2012--13 location comes from the documented
2011--12 special-education patch. Whether each vacated building stayed empty
later is not recorded.
