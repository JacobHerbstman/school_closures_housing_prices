\section*{September 22, 2026: Refreshing the county and CPI downloads}
\textit{Drafted by Claude; Jacob requested the refresh.}

All four acquisitions were downloaded again on September 22 after their
scripts were revised. The revised scripts use literal years and publish only
a complete, checked extract, so a failed transfer keeps the previous file. The
three county extracts are byte-identical to the September 7 versions: parcel
sales (1,021,374 records), residential improvements (406,795 records), and
historical parcel coordinates (399,882 PIN-years). The Chicago CPI series
gained one observation, August 2026, and no earlier value changed. Every
derived production file is therefore unchanged, including the master
transactions, both clean-sales files and the exposure file.

The September 7 snapshots are preserved byte-for-byte, with checksums, in
\texttt{data\_raw/county\_snapshots\_2026-09-07/}. Each acquisition README now
records the retrieval date and SHA-256 of its current file. A later refresh
may change the county data: the Assessor revises sales and characteristics,
and 2025 sales were still being recorded. Such a refresh should be compared
against these checksums before any estimate is updated.
