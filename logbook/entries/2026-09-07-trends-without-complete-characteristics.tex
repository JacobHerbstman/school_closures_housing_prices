\section*{September 7, 2026: Retaining incomplete records in raw price trends}
\textit{Codex draft; descriptive-sample change requested by Jacob.}

Descriptive price trends now retain transactions with missing property
characteristics. The sample grows from 10,921 to 11,600 sales: 3,517 near
selected closed schools and 8,083 near controls. All previously selected sales
remain. Of the 679 added sales, 673 have blank apartment-unit counts; six
additional sales lack other required characteristics. Property classification
still uses the assessor's recorded category.

The 30/49 school roster, quarter-mile geography, transaction weights, and
property-eligibility rules remain fixed. Room consistency checks apply where
both counts are observed. Price-per-square-foot screens retain the existing
annual cutoffs and apply where positive floor area is observed. Five control
sales without usable floor area enter dollar-price summaries but not average
building size. Existing regressions retain their complete-characteristics sample.

Including these sales lowers 2018 mean prices near closed schools from about
\$307,000 to \$294,000 and medians from \$181,000 to \$172,000, in 2022 dollars.
Control means fall from \$234,000 to \$230,000 and medians from \$143,000 to
\$139,000. The plotted prices describe the broader set of transactions sold.
