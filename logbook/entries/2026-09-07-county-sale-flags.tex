\section*{September 7, 2026: What the county sale flags remove}
\textit{Drafted by Codex from the saved county extract and published county SQL.}

Most records excluded by the county sale flags have very low recorded prices.
Within the fixed school comparison, the flags remove 1,795 of 5,551 records
near closed schools and 3,666 of 12,102 near schools that stayed open.
Prices at or below \$10,000 account for 90.5\% and 89.1\% of these losses.
Of those low-price records, 1,169 near closed schools and 2,473 near controls
record exactly \$0 or \$1. These recorded amounts need not represent the
market value of the property.

The table allocates overlapping exclusions to low price first, then deed
type, then repeat records. The allocation changes no sample membership.
The remaining deed exclusions concern quitclaim and executor instruments in
this extract. Such transfers can include genuine transactions, so their
exclusion is a substantive restriction rather than proof of recording error.

The code applies all three flags in the correct direction. Every flag in
the 428,582-record master matches the saved county source by transaction ID,
and every corrected record preserves those flags. None is missing.
The recorded-price rule matches the county flag for every master record.
Two descriptions needed correction: the low-price rule includes exactly
\$10,000, and the repeat rule concerns the same parcel \emph{at the same price}
within 365 days. It does not exclude every property resold within a year.

The county computes repeats before removing some duplicate records and
substituting MyDec dates. The released export cannot independently reconstruct
all those source histories. Blank exported deed types also conceal the
source distinction between an empty string and SQL NULL. The audit verifies
faithful use of the published flags without claiming to validate every
underlying deed. Jacob confirmed retaining these deed exclusions and the current cleaning as the working version. No filter changed; the descriptive sample remains 11,600 sales.
