\section*{September 9, 2026: Half-mile single-family comparison}
\textit{Drafted by Codex.}

At Jacob's request, the single-family packet and annual event studies now
also use a half-mile radius for 2008--2018. The same radius applies to
receiving-school, other-candidate, and treatment-control overlap exclusions.
School definitions, property classes, price-per-square-foot cutoffs,
inflation adjustment, and transaction weights remain fixed.

The descriptive sample grows from 5,645 to 11,206 sales. Most of the gain is
in controls: treated sales rise from 1,461 to 1,686 and control sales from
4,184 to 9,520. The wider geography adds 7,621 sales and excludes 2,060 that
entered at a quarter mile. Ten of the 78 school sites have no retained sales,
compared with three previously. Treatment-control overlap is 1,485 of 19,153
otherwise eligible sales near either group (7.75\%). More transactions do not
mean more school neighborhoods are represented.

The event studies use 11,202 complete-record sales at 68 sites. With property
controls, the 2018 dollar estimate is \$33,344 (95\% interval: $-\$38,675$
to \$105,362) and the log estimate is $-0.130$ (interval: $-0.340$ to $0.080$).
The joint pre-period test rejects zero coefficients in the hedonic log model
($p=0.031$). The larger sample does not resolve identification concerns.
