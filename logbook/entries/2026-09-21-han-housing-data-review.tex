\section*{September 21, 2026: What the Seattle housing paper suggests checking next}
\textit{Drafted by Codex; recommendations, not adopted specification changes.}

The current Chicago results do not establish a precise zero. With transaction
weights and property controls, the pooled half-mile single-family log estimate
is 3.0\%, with a 95\% interval of $[-12.2\%,20.8\%]$. Equal site-year weights
and removing the busiest three treated sites increase the estimates. These
differences leave uncertainty about which neighborhoods drive the comparison;
pre-period price movements remain a separate identification concern.

Reading Han and Idoux's November 12, 2025 Seattle paper identifies two useful
differences from our models: a fixed pre-reform assessment for each property
and property-characteristic coefficients that vary by period (pp. 12--13).
Our earlier initial-price adjustment instead used school-area pre-period sale
means, on the older quarter-mile all-home sample. Current half-mile models
hold the characteristic coefficients fixed over time. The paper's composition
tests also treat characteristics of sold houses as outcomes (Table A.1).

The paper does not provide a complete transaction-cleaning recipe. Bishop et
al.'s cited best-practices review emphasizes documenting restrictions and their
consequences, without prescribing a universal outlier cutoff (2020, p. 266).
Our county flags, property restrictions, price checks, and upper-tail trim are
explicit, but do not identify every related-party, foreclosure, or investor
sale. The existing seller-name audit remains a proxy analysis. Requiring
complete characteristics removes only four of the current packet's 11,206
sales, all controls; it is not a substantial source of current sample loss.

The recommended next checks are changes in the single-family sale mix,
characteristic-by-year price controls on the unchanged sample, and repeat sales
of the same parcels. For repeat sales, compare both the current model and
parcel fixed effects on the same restricted sample; report pre/post support
and recorded improvements. This separates sample selection from adjustment
for fixed property quality. Historical parcel assessments and a true parcel
stock denominator merit a separate feasibility check. The existing coordinate
extract contains sold PIN-years, so it cannot yet supply turnover denominators.
None of these checks, even if estimates stabilize, repairs closure selection
or anticipatory price changes by itself.
