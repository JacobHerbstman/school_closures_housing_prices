\section*{September 8, 2026: Housing trends at three distances}
\textit{Drafted by Codex.}

The descriptive comparison retains Noah's 30 treated and 49 control school
programs. The overview now uses three cumulative distances from school
buildings: one-eighth, one-quarter, and one-half mile. Each packet contains
the same ten pages, with transaction weights, price screens, and school
classifications held fixed. Missing property characteristics remain allowed.

Treatment-control overlap is small at a quarter mile but appreciable at a
half mile. The denominator below contains sales passing the descriptive price
rules within the stated distance of either group, before geographic exclusions.
Each transaction counts once, including transactions near both groups.

\begin{center}
\begin{tabular}{lrrrrr}
Radius & Near either & Near both & Overlap & Retained treated & Retained control\\
\hline
1/8 mile & 3,663 & 3 & 0.08\% & 1,329 & 2,293\\
1/4 mile & 13,343 & 365 & 2.74\% & 3,517 & 8,083\\
1/2 mile & 37,432 & 4,699 & 12.55\% & 3,237 & 15,387\\
\end{tabular}
\end{center}

All nearby-school exclusions expand with the selected radius. Sales near both
groups are excluded, as are sales near receiving schools or February
candidates outside the 30/49 comparison. Same-group overlaps enter once at the
nearest school site. Consequently, these retained samples need not be nested:
the half-mile sample has fewer treated-area sales than the quarter-mile sample,
despite covering a larger area. Differences between the packets combine a
change in distance with a change in the mix of neighborhoods represented.

The quarter-mile packet reproduces the prior 11,600 transaction IDs and
annual price summaries exactly. The alternatives contain 3,622 and 18,624
sales. These are descriptive sensitivity checks; they do not replace the
working quarter-mile comparison or change any regression specification.
