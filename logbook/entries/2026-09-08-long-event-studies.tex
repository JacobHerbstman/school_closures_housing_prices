\section*{September 8, 2026: Annual event studies through 2023}
\textit{Drafted by Codex.}

The extended quarter-mile event studies use 17,212 sales at 77 school sites
in 2008--2023. Each transaction receives equal weight. The regressions retain
the existing complete-characteristics requirement, school-site and year
fixed effects, 2012 reference year, and school-site clustered intervals.
The complete-characteristics sample matches the extended overview; refitting
on 2008--2018 reproduces all original annual event coefficients.

Dollar estimates remain mostly positive after 2013, while log estimates are
near zero or negative. With property controls, the 2023 estimate is
\$44,990 (95\% interval: $-\$17,058$ to \$107,037); the log estimate is
$-0.106$ (interval: $-0.339$ to $0.127$). These estimates do not resolve
the earlier concerns about anticipation, pre-trends, or changing sale composition.
